THE BORING BUT IMPORTANT BIT

Shipping & returns.

Short, honest and croc-approved.

Delivery

  • Most orders reach you in 4-5 days. Remote pin codes can take longer.
  • Free delivery on orders above Rs. 999. Below that, a flat Rs. 79.
  • Posters ship in tamper-proof packaging so they arrive the way we printed them.
  • Gift cards are digital and arrive by email.

Payment

UPI, cards, netbanking and Cash on Delivery. Pay however keeps you happiest.

Returns

  • Damaged, wrong or misprinted? Write to us within 7 days of delivery with a photo.
  • We will reprint or refund, your pick.
  • Posters are limited editions, so we cannot take back a poster that arrived perfectly and just did not match the curtains.
  • Gift cards are not refundable.
